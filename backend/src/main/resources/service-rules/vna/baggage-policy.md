# Vietnam Airlines - Baggage Policy

## Carry-on Baggage

### Economy Class
- **Weight Limit**: 7 kg
- **Size Limit**: 56 x 36 x 23 cm
- **Number of Pieces**: 1 piece
- **Additional Items**: 
  - 1 personal item (handbag, laptop bag) max 40 x 30 x 15 cm
  - 1 coat or blanket
  - Reading material for the flight

### Business Class
- **Weight Limit**: 14 kg (or 2 pieces x 7 kg each)
- **Size Limit**: 56 x 36 x 23 cm per piece
- **Number of Pieces**: 2 pieces
- **Additional Items**: Same as Economy Class

### First Class (International)
- **Weight Limit**: 14 kg (or 2 pieces x 7 kg each)
- **Size Limit**: 56 x 36 x 23 cm per piece
- **Number of Pieces**: 2 pieces
- **Additional Items**: Same as Economy Class

## Checked Baggage

### Economy Class - Domestic
- **Weight Limit**: 23 kg per piece
- **Size Limit**: Sum of 3 dimensions ≤ 158 cm
- **Free Allowance**: 1 piece (for Saver fare) or 2 pieces (for Flex fare)
- **Additional Fee**: 200,000 VND per kg (over allowance)

### Economy Class - International
- **Weight Limit**: 23 kg per piece
- **Size Limit**: Sum of 3 dimensions ≤ 158 cm
- **Free Allowance**: 
  - Asia routes: 1 piece (Saver) or 2 pieces (Flex)
  - Europe/US routes: 2 pieces (Saver) or 2 pieces (Flex)
- **Additional Fee**: Varies by route, typically $50-100 USD per additional piece

### Business Class
- **Weight Limit**: 32 kg per piece
- **Size Limit**: Sum of 3 dimensions ≤ 158 cm
- **Free Allowance**: 2 pieces (domestic) or 2-3 pieces (international)
- **Additional Fee**: Reduced rates compared to Economy

## Special Items

### Sports Equipment
- **Golf Equipment**: Free as part of checked baggage allowance (1 golf bag = 1 piece)
- **Surfboard**: Up to 2.5m length, extra fee applies
- **Bicycle**: Must be packed in hard case, counts as 1 checked piece
- **Skiing Equipment**: 1 set of skis/snowboard + boots = 1 piece

### Musical Instruments
- **Small Instruments** (violin, guitar): Can be carry-on if within size limits
- **Large Instruments**: Must purchase extra seat or check as baggage
- **Cello/Double Bass**: Requires advance notice and extra seat purchase

### Medical Equipment
- **Wheelchairs**: Free, does not count towards baggage allowance
- **CPAP/Oxygen**: Allowed in cabin with medical certificate
- **Crutches/Walkers**: Free, does not count towards allowance

## Infant Baggage

### Children under 2 years
- **Carry-on**: 1 diaper bag up to 5 kg
- **Checked**: 1 piece up to 10 kg
- **Stroller**: Free, can be checked at gate

### Children 2-11 years
- Same allowance as adult passenger

## Excess Baggage Fees

### Domestic Routes
- **1-5 kg over**: 100,000 VND
- **6-15 kg over**: 300,000 VND
- **16+ kg over**: 200,000 VND per kg

### International Routes
- **Asia**: $20-40 USD per kg
- **Europe**: $40-60 USD per kg
- **USA**: $100-150 USD per additional piece

## Prohibited Items

### Strictly Prohibited
- Explosives and flammable materials
- Compressed gases (except medical oxygen)
- Corrosive materials
- Radioactive materials
- Poisons and infectious substances
- Weapons and ammunition (except with permit)

### Restricted in Cabin
- Liquids over 100ml (must be checked)
- Sharp objects (scissors, knives)
- Tools (screwdrivers, wrenches)
- Sports equipment (baseball bats, hockey sticks)

### Battery Restrictions
- Power banks: Max 100Wh (carry-on only)
- Spare lithium batteries: Carry-on only
- E-cigarettes: Carry-on only, cannot be used on board

## Special Baggage Services

### Fragile Items
- Must be declared at check-in
- Additional fee: 100,000 VND
- Limited liability coverage

### Valuable Items
- Recommended to carry in cabin
- If checked, declare value at check-in
- Additional insurance available
